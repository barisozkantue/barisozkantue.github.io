How Supervision Works

You lead your thesis from start to finish. What changes is how I support you: first as a co-pilot reading the map with you, later by answering your questions. The research proposal is where that shift happens, which is why I treat it as the most important milestone of your thesis.

1
Before the proposal Co-pilot
Proactive and directive
"Where am I heading, and what is coming up?"

You are in the driver's seat from day one. Before the proposal, I am your co-pilot: I look at the map, give you heads-ups about what is coming, and suggest directions when the road forks. The aim of this stretch is simple: get you to a solid proposal.

What to expect

Heads-ups: pitfalls, scope traps and literature you should not miss
Directions: concrete suggestions on topic, focus and next steps, with honest feedback on early drafts

Your part

Keep driving and bring drafts early, even rough ones. A co-pilot can only give directions when there is something on paper to look at.
2
The milestone 2–4 pages
The research proposal
"Do we both know what you will do next, and why?"

The proposal is where the route we explored together becomes your map. Once we agree on it, my support switches from giving directions to answering your questions. Keep it compact: two to four pages that show your problem, your approach and how they fit together.

Why this is the key milestone

It is where my support changes. With the route on paper, you no longer need a co-pilot reading the map for you.
It is our shared reference. Later feedback is measured against what the proposal says you will do.
It is the blueprint of your thesis. A clear proposal makes every later chapter easier to write.
3
After the proposal On request
Reactive and responsive
"What do I need to ask my supervisor?"

With an agreed proposal, you have your own map. I answer your questions and respond to what you bring: drafts, results and problems. You keep driving; I am there when you need a second opinion.

What to expect

Answers, not directions: feedback on the questions and work you bring to me
Room to decide: day-to-day choices are yours, within the plan

Your part

Set the agenda for our meetings. When something changes — data, method, company expectations — bring the proposal back to the table and we revise it together.
